What's Included in Registration

Conference registration includes all workshops and tutorials, main conference speakers and technical sessions, meals and coffee breaks, and one ticket to the evening poster session and conference dinner at the Cleveland Museum of Natural History. Additional dinner tickets can be purchased through the registration website.
